**Superstore Profitability Analysis Dashboard | Tableau**



An interactive Tableau dashboard uncovering why high sales do not always translate into high profit in a U.S. retail superstore — with aggressive discounting identified as the primary profitability challenge.

Key Findings

•	Discounts are reducing profitability: Orders with 0% discount generated approximately +29.5% profit margin, while 30%+ discount orders resulted in negative margins (\~ -16.5%).

•	Tables sub-category is loss-making despite high sales due to heavy discounting (\~26%).

•	South region underperforms, while Technology demonstrates stronger profitability potential.

**Dataset Description**

Dataset Source: Sample Superstore Dataset

Dataset Scope: U.S. retail sales and order data including Sales, Profit, Discount, Region, Segment, Category, State, and Shipping metrics.



Data Preparation: Country field excluded (single-country dataset with no variation). Calculated fields used for profit margin and profitability analysis.
